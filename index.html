Bahian expressions you may hear in Salvador

Expressions that help you understand everyday conversations, local humor and rhythm.

Oxente

An expression of surprise, amazement or emphasis. It can mean “wow,” “really?” or “what do you mean?”

Example: “Oxente, are you leaving already?”

Ó paí, ó

An expression similar to “look at that” or “check this out.” It is used to draw attention to something happening nearby.

Example: “Ó paí, ó! The band is starting.”

Lá ele

A humorous response used to reject an ambiguous or suggestive phrase and distance oneself from the double meaning.

Example: Someone says something with a double meaning and the response is: “Lá ele!”
