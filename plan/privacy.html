Privacy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. This page explains how To Know Salvador, a local travel team in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil, handles the information you send through this page.

What we collect

  • What you type in the form: your name, WhatsApp number, travel dates, number of travelers, interests and, if you choose, your budget.
  • How you reached the page: ad campaign details and the Google click ID in the page address.
  • If you accept cookies: anonymous usage data from Google Analytics and Google Ads.

Why

  • To prepare your trip quote and talk to you about it on WhatsApp.
  • To understand which ads bring travelers to us, so we spend less on the ones that don't.

We never sell your information, and we only share what a partner needs to deliver a booking you approved.

Where it is stored

Your form answers are stored in a private Google Sheet used only by our team. Analytics and ad data are processed by Google.

How long we keep it

We delete your form answers 12 months after our last conversation with you.

Cookies

Visitors in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland are asked before any analytics or advertising cookies are used. You can decline and the page works the same.

Your choices
