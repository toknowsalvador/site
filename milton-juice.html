🥥 Milton's Juice

A flavor of Salvador

What is this juice?

A refreshing local drink made by Milton with lime, dried coconut and a little sugar — creamy, citrusy and unlike anything else in the Historic Center.

Made by Milton in Salvador's Historic Center
Experiences recommended by Milton

The story behind the flavor

Simple ingredients. A well-kept secret.

Milton reveals only the essentials: the drink contains lime, dried coconut and a little sugar. He prepares it himself and keeps it cold in eight-liter thermal bottles.

Contains sugar.

  • 🍋
    Fresh and creamy

    The coconut softens the lime's acidity and creates a smooth, distinctive texture.

  • 🔐
    The exact recipe stays secret

    The proportions and preparation method are part of Milton's signature.

  • 🤍
    A white coconut drink

    Despite the lime, the coconut gives the juice its characteristic light color.
