## Proposed negatives (round 3)

Team decision 2026-10-20: Spanish searches may be excluded too (focus on travelers paying in USD/EUR/GBP), so shared Portuguese/Spanish words now go in as broad negatives:

- Portuguese/Spanish: `turismo`, `lugares`, `agência`, `agencia`, `visitar`, `visitando`, `excursões`
- Spanish: `excursiones`, `hacer`, `conocer`, `que`, `donde`, `mejor`, `mejores`, `playa`, `playas`, `paseo`, `paseos`, `buceo`, `noche`
- French/Italian: `faire`, `voir`, `cosa`, `fare`
- Not our offer: `helicóptero`, `helicoptero`, `helicopter`, `"lonely planet"`

Left out: `de bahia` / `salvador de bahia` (English speakers use it), `excursion` (English), `tour`/`tours`, `praia` ("Praia do Forte" day trip; impressions only, no clicks).
