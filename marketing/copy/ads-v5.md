# Ads v5 — Google Search RSAs

Only the **Things to do** block changes (EXP-001); Trip planner, Tours & transfers and Family & group trips are identical to v4.

Things to do changes:
- Removed "Salvador Things to Do List" and "What to Do in Salvador?" — they read as a free list/guide and attract research clicks.
- Removed "Free Custom Quote in 24h" and "We Speak English & Spanish" (Spanish searches are now excluded); the quote line no longer says "free" ("Quote on WhatsApp in 24h"); "pay nothing until you approve" stays as the risk reversal.
- Added "Salvador Bahia Tours" (4 of 5 leads came from that keyword), "One Tour or Your Whole Trip" and "Add Transfers, Shows & More" (tour first, package as add-on), "Afro-Bahian Culture Tours" (what our guides lead, per landing v10).
- Review pass (same day): "Your Salvador Days, Planned" → "Your Local Guide in Salvador" (no headline matched the live keyword "salvador bahia guide"); "Salvador Tours & Shows" → "Salvador Tours & Day Trips" ("shows" was in 3 headlines; day trips are higher-value tours run by our team). "Walking tour" was considered and rejected: in Pelourinho it reads as the free walking tour.
- Descriptions now open with the tour and offer the rest as optional. Path `things-to-do` → `tours`.
- No price and no "private tours" (team, 2026-10-24).

Message match: every group promises the same thing as the landing hero (planned and arranged by locals, free quote, pay only after approval). Proof claim ("Rated 5.0 on Tripadvisor") is verified in brief `## Proof`; other platforms are never named (team decision 2026-10-09); re-check before each new version.
